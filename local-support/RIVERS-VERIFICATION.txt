Rivers and water moats — November 11, 2026

Implementation
  A villager specifies a line 1/3/5 tiles wide or a rectangular area.
  Rivers and water moats fill with water as their tiles are completed.
  Drag direction determines the visible river flow.
  Ships can use sufficiently wide rivers; water moats are closed to ships.
  Both work types block land movement. Shared work, Shift queues, cancellation
  with unused-cost refunds, and restoration are supported.
  Obstacle relocation, waiting for free space, and building protection are retained.
  Tiles under buildings are skipped, so a channel near a building may have gaps.
  Water stays at a fixed level; water spreading and flooding are not simulated.
