Implemented
  C++20, hotkey and AI vocabulary fixes for Russian game data.
  Compatibility checks for game data, simulation revision, and the worldcraft package.
  Individual, line, and area tree planting; growth and ordinary woodcutting.
  Shared villager tasks: payment, Shift queues, multiple workers, cancellation,
  land reservation, saving, replaying, and network commands.
  Earthworks panel: three heights and five pairs of line/area buttons.
  Mountains with slopes, dry moats, shallow ditches, lakes, and terrain restoration.
  A mound or pit marks the work site instead of a stump.
  Creatures, trees, resources, and natural cliffs move to suitable free space
  when work completes. There is no separate move-away animation.
  Health, ownership, entity identity, and remaining resources are preserved.
  Multi-tile cliffs move as a whole, freeing their entire previous footprint.
  Dry earthworks replace water with sand. Restoration returns the original
  elevation and terrain type, including water.
  Work is allowed right next to buildings; foundations and shared vertices are protected.
  Paths, combat height, and terrain visibility update when work completes.
  Fog of war does not reveal hidden elevation or terrain-type changes.

Default parameters
  Tree: 10 wood, 10 seconds of work for one villager, 60 seconds of growth.
  Raise heights: hill 4, mountain 12 (default), tall mountain 18 levels.
  Editable elevation range: -4 to 24; repeating an order raises a mountain further.
  Slopes form within six tiles of the selected tile and are included in the price.
  Mountain: 10 wood and 15 seconds of work per raised level of the selected tile.
  Dry moat, lake, and restoration: 5 wood and 10 seconds per changed level.
  Shallow ditch: 3 wood and 5 seconds per changed level.
  A two-level dry moat is impassable; a one-level ditch is traversable.
  A lake creates water tiles at elevation -2 without flow simulation.
  Up to 256 selected tiles per order, sites per player, and tasks per villager.
  The site must be explored; the central tile must be visible when ordering.
  Relocation searches within 32 tiles. If even one object has no free space,
  all changes wait; another attempt is made after one second.
  Up to two earthwork completion attempts are made per tick.
  Mod settings: mods/worldcraft; original game assets are unchanged.
