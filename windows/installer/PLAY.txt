OpenAoK Worldcraft 0.4.0

Launch
  Open the OpenAoK Worldcraft shortcut on the desktop or in the Start menu.
  You can also run Play-OpenAoK.cmd from the installation folder.
  Python, Visual Studio, and other development tools are not required.

Worldcraft interface language
  Choose English or Русский on the Worldcraft language page during installation.
  The game remembers this choice for shortcuts, Play-OpenAoK.cmd, and the EXE.
  No language option is needed when starting the installed game. Re-running
  the installer lets you change the saved choice; it starts with your last selection.
  This changes only Worldcraft additions; the original game's language
  comes from your game data. Russian instructions are in PLAY_ru.txt.
  For a one-time override, use --worldcraft-language en or --worldcraft-language ru.

Planting forests
  Select one or more villagers.
  In the second command-panel row, press the line of three trees or tree square.
  Hold the left mouse button on the map, drag, and release.
  One click plants one tree. Shift adds to the queue; Esc exits placement.
  The cross cancels unfinished work in the selected villagers' queues.
  A tree costs 10 wood, 10 seconds of work for one villager, and 60 seconds of growth.
  Fully grown trees are chopped with the ordinary command.

Multiplayer
  Install the same package on both computers.
  One player creates a match; the other connects using the host's local IP address.
  If Windows Firewall asks, allow the game on your private network.

Mountains, dry moats, ditches, and lakes
  Select villagers. The fourth button in the second row opens earthworks.
  The first three buttons choose height: hill, mountain, or tall mountain.
  The default mountain is roughly as tall as a tower or castle.
  Next come button pairs: mountain, dry moat, terrain restoration, ditch, and lake.
  A row of icons selects a line; a square of icons selects a rectangular area.
  The last two buttons cancel work and return to the normal panel.
  Drag over the site. Shift queues work; the cross cancels unfinished tasks
  and refunds the unused part of their cost.
  Slopes around the selected site are created automatically and included in the price.
  Repeating an order increases height, up to 24 levels.
  Gentle slopes are traversable; steep slopes and dry moats are barriers.
  Restore terrain to fill a moat or return a mountain to its original surface.
  The cross does not undo completed tiles.
  Cost per tile level: mountain — 10 wood and 15 seconds of work;
  dry moat, lake, and restoration — 5 wood and 10 seconds;
  shallow ditch — 3 wood and 5 seconds. Multiple villagers speed up work.
  A dry moat is two levels below the surface.
  The site must be explored. Creatures, trees, and resources move to free space;
  work waits if no space is available. Dry earthworks replace water with sand.
  Mountains can be built right next to buildings: foundations are preserved.
  Up to 256 selected tiles per order, plus automatic slopes.
  A one-level ditch is traversable. A lake creates water at elevation -2.

Rivers and water moats
  Select a villager and the second button in the third command row.
  Choose line width 1/3/5 tiles, then river or moat, line or area.
  Drag out a channel. Completed tiles fill with water immediately.
  River flow is shown from the line's start to its end; it does not change ship speed.
  Ships can use wide rivers. Water moats also block ships.
  Land units cannot cross either rivers or water moats.
  The bottom menu row restores original terrain, cancels work, and exits.
  Width applies to lines; drag to determine an area's size.
  On flat ground, a tile costs 10 wood and 20 seconds of work for one villager.

Walking Tree
  The first button in the villager's third row grows a treant: 200 wood,
  50 gold, 60 seconds of work, and 3 population slots.
  A new random map has 10 peaceful wild treants. Bring a unit within
  three tiles and the treant will join you permanently.

Saves
  Saves and the profile are stored in %APPDATA%\OpenAoK, separate from the game.
  Uninstallation preserves them. Old saves from earlier engine versions
  are not automatically converted.

Status of this version
  Forest growing, woodcutting, mountains with slopes, dry moats, terrain
  restoration, shallow ditches, lakes, rivers with visible flow, water moats,
  and treants are available.
  Known issues include delayed AI development and the missing bonus DEMO.SCN.

Package
  This is a local build using game data supplied by the user.
  A single installer file is enough to transfer it to your computer.
  Age of Empires II files and fonts retain their owners' rights;
  the engine license and library notices are in the installation folder.
  Engine source: https://github.com/AppleVetal/AgeOfEmpire2-openaok
